Now, you have gathered some information about Shrek's Swamp:


First, is that there are slimy tentacles which pull you down and transport you to a different location. However, the second time they pull you down, you WON'T come back up.

Second, is that if you feel the ground hardening under you, it does not mean you have reached land. In fact, too much walking around that area will cause it collapse, leaving you to the mercy of the black abyss.


Finally, remember that no one has managed to document Shrek as he is a monster beyond reason. Just try not to lose your wits at the sight -- or smell -- of him. Farewell, and may the will of Shaggy be with you!
